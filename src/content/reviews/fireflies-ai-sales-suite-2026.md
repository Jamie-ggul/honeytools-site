## What's Actually in the Sales Suite

Six pieces, according to the launch materials:

**Deal Intelligence** — flags at-risk deals, competitor mentions, and missing stakeholders based on call and CRM data.

**CRM Autofill** — automatically populates deal fields in Salesforce or HubSpot from call content, instead of a rep manually logging notes after every call.

**AI Scorecards** — scores deals against established sales methodologies (MEDDIC, BANT, SPICED) automatically, rather than a manager manually reviewing calls to fill these out.

**Meeting Prep** — generates prep material ahead of a call using prior call history, email threads, and CRM records.

**Sales Assist** — real-time support during a call for strategy, pricing questions, and objection handling.

**AskFred (sales context)** — natural-language Q&A specifically over deal and rep data, an extension of the general AskFred feature covered in the [main Fireflies.ai review](/reviews/fireflies-ai-review-2026/).

## Where This Sits in the Pricing

Deal Intelligence and the sales-suite features are positioned as part of the **Business and Enterprise plans** — not a separate add-on purchase on top of those tiers, based on the launch materials. ## The Gong Comparison, With Real Numbers

This is the part worth being specific about, because "a fraction of the price" is a big claim. Gong doesn't publish pricing — it's sold entirely through custom sales quotes — but based on third-party procurement data, per-seat pricing typically runs **$1,200–$2,400 per seat, per year**, with contracts usually requiring a minimum of 10–15 seats. A 50-seat, one-year Gong contract commonly lands somewhere around $70,000–$110,000.

Fireflies' Business plan, by comparison, runs **$228/seat/year** billed annually ($19 × 12). Even Enterprise at $39/seat/month annually comes to $468/seat/year — still a small fraction of Gong's typical per-seat cost. For a 50-seat team, that's the difference between roughly $11,400–$23,400/year on Fireflies versus $70,000+ on Gong.

That gap is real and well-documented on the Gong side. What's genuinely unverifiable from the outside — and this is the honest caveat — is whether Fireflies' AI Sales Suite matches Gong's deal-scoring and coaching accuracy after years of Gong refining that exact category. A lower price for a comparable feature list isn't automatically a lower price for comparable *results*. That's the kind of thing only a trial against your own sales calls would actually answer.
