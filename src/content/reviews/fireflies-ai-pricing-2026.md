## Fireflies.ai Free: What You Actually Get

The free plan isn't a time-limited trial — it's a permanent tier. It includes unlimited transcription and unlimited AI summaries, which is more generous than it sounds, since some competitors gate transcription itself behind a paywall. The catch is storage: 400 minutes **per team**, not per person. Video recording isn't included on free at all — audio transcription only.

That "per team" detail matters more than it looks. If you're the only one using Fireflies, 400 minutes covers a handful of meetings a month. If three or four teammates are all recording their own calls into the same team, that same 400-minute pool empties out fast — often within the first couple of weeks.

## Fireflies.ai Pro: What Changes

Pro adds:

- 8,000 minutes of storage per seat (a big jump from the shared 400-minute pool)
- Video recording, capped at 2 hours per meeting
- 30 AI credits instead of 20
- A personal AI assistant, email assistant, and voice agent features

This is the tier that makes sense once storage — not features — becomes the actual blocker on free.

## Fireflies.ai Business: What Changes

Business adds:

- Unlimited storage (no minutes cap at all)
- Video recording, capped at 3 hours per meeting
- Conversation intelligence and team-level analytics
- Multi-language mode for teams that meet in more than one language

Business is the plan most likely to fit a team with a real, ongoing meeting cadence — the unlimited storage alone removes the thing most likely to force an upgrade later.

## The Pricing Detail Most People Miss

Fireflies' pricing page leads with the annual rate: Pro at **$10/seat/month**, Business at **$19/seat/month**. Those numbers only apply if you pay for the year upfront. Billed monthly instead, Pro jumps to **$18/seat/month** and Business to **$29/seat/month** — roughly an 80% and 53% increase per seat, respectively, for the exact same features.

Neither price is hidden — both are shown on the pricing page — but the annual figure is the one that's easiest to remember and the monthly figure is the one you'll actually see on a card statement if you don't commit to the year. For a 5-person team, that gap is the difference between $50/month and $90/month on Pro alone. Worth deciding upfront which billing cycle you actually want, rather than anchoring on the smaller number.

Enterprise, notably, skips this entirely — it's **annual billing only**, no monthly option at $39/seat/month.

## Is Free Enough?

For a single person testing whether an AI notetaker fits their workflow at all, yes — the free tier's unlimited transcription and summaries are enough to get a real sense of it, as long as you're mindful of the 400-minute team pool. For any team where more than one or two people are recording regularly, free empties out quickly enough that it functions more like an extended trial than a long-term plan, even though it's not technically time-limited.

## Fireflies.ai Business and Enterprise (Briefly)

Above Business, Enterprise adds SSO, SCIM provisioning, HIPAA compliance, audit logs, and a dedicated account manager — relevant mainly for larger organizations with compliance requirements (healthcare, for example, given the HIPAA angle) or IT departments that need centralized user management. If you're evaluating Enterprise, you're likely already talking to Fireflies' sales team rather than comparing plans on a pricing page.

## FAQ

**Is the free plan actually usable long-term, or is it just a trial?**
It's genuinely permanent, not time-limited — but the shared 400-minutes-per-team cap means it behaves like a trial for any team beyond a single regular user.

**Does the AI credit limit affect the summaries I get after every meeting?**
Fireflies advertises "unlimited AI summaries" across all plans — the AI credits are a separate allowance tied to other AI features (like the assistant tools), not the core post-meeting summary.

**Is it cheaper to pay monthly or annually?**
Annually, by a wide margin — Pro is 80% more expensive per seat billed monthly ($18 vs. $10), and Business is about 53% more ($29 vs. $19). If you're fairly confident you'll keep using it, the annual commitment pays for itself quickly.

## Final Verdict

Start on free and actually hit its limits before paying for anything — the 400-minute team pool will tell you fast whether this fits your workflow. Once storage becomes the real constraint, Pro or Business is a straightforward upgrade, but decide on monthly vs. annual billing deliberately, since it's a meaningfully different price for the same plan.
