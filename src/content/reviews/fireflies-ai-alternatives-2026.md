Fathom leans hardest into a genuinely generous free tier, tl;dv splits cleanly between a usable free plan and sales-focused paid tiers, Avoma bundles in deal intelligence the way Fireflies' own Sales Suite does, Granola skips the meeting bot entirely, MeetGeek pushes further on multilingual support, and Grain is built around customer-success teams sharing call moments. ## Why You'd Look for an Alternative at All

Fireflies is a strong generalist — broad language support, wide integrations, and now a real sales-intelligence layer on top. But "generalist" has an edge too: if your actual complaint is a hard dollar limit, a specific team function (recruiting, customer success), discomfort with a visible bot joining calls, or a workflow Fireflies just doesn't optimize for, a more specialized tool can be a better fit than a broader one, even a good one.

## The Alternatives

**Fathom** — the strongest option if budget is the real constraint. Its free plan includes unlimited recordings and AI summaries for individual use, which is unusually generous compared to the storage-capped free tiers most competitors (Fireflies included) offer. Team plan starts around $15/seat/month, Business around $25/seat/month. Worth it if you're a solo user or very small team that doesn't need CRM integrations or team-wide analytics.

**tl;dv** — splits cleanly into a real free tier and a sales-heavy paid side. Free gives unlimited recordings in 30+ languages, but caps you at 10 AI summaries for the *lifetime* of the account and auto-deletes recordings after 3 months — worth knowing before you lean on it long-term. Pro runs about $18/seat/month (annual), and Business (~$59/seat/month annual) adds native Salesforce/HubSpot sync and sales coaching tools similar to what Fireflies' Sales Suite offers. Worth it if you want to trial a generous free tier first and only pay once you hit a real limit.

**Avoma** — the most direct alternative to Fireflies for revenue teams, since it bundles meeting notes with conversation intelligence and deal insights from the start rather than as a bolt-on. Startup plan from $19/seat/month, Organization plan from $29/seat/month — both cheaper than Fireflies' Business tier while covering similar sales-intelligence ground. Worth it if the [Fireflies AI Sales Suite](/reviews/fireflies-ai-sales-suite-2026/) is the feature you actually want, and you'd rather have it built-in from a lower price point than layered onto a general notetaker.

**Granola** — the odd one out, and worth knowing about specifically because it skips the bot. Instead of a visible AI participant joining your call, Granola runs locally and enhances your own live notes afterward — a meaningfully different privacy posture for teams uncomfortable with a bot recording every meeting. Business plan from $14/seat/month, Enterprise from $35/seat/month. Worth it if the idea of an AI notetaker joining calls (something we get into in more detail in [our Fireflies privacy breakdown](/reviews/fireflies-ai-privacy-security-2026/)) is itself the objection, not the price or features.

**MeetGeek** — pushes further on multilingual support, which is notable given that's already one of Fireflies' stronger features. Free plan available, Pro from $9.99/seat/month, Business from $17/seat/month — meaningfully cheaper than Fireflies at every tier. Worth checking if your team meets in several languages and price is a bigger factor than Fireflies' broader integration list.

**Grain** — built around customer-success and customer-facing teams sharing specific call moments (clips, highlights) rather than full transcripts. Free plan available, Starter from $15/seat/month, Business from $29/seat/month. Worth it if your primary use case is pulling customer quotes and moments for internal sharing rather than full meeting documentation.

**Otter.ai** is the other alternative people ask about most — we cover that comparison in full detail separately since it deserves its own breakdown: see [Fireflies.ai vs. Otter.ai](/reviews/fireflies-ai-vs-otter-ai/).

## FAQ

**Is any of these actually better than Fireflies overall?**
Not across the board — each one wins on a specific axis. Fathom and MeetGeek win on price, Avoma on out-of-the-box sales intelligence, Granola on not using a visible bot, Grain on customer-success-specific sharing. None is a strict upgrade over Fireflies in every category.

**Can I trial more than one before deciding?**
Yes, and given how generous some of these free tiers are (particularly Fathom's), it's worth running two or three on real meetings for a week before committing to a paid plan anywhere.

**Which of these is closest to a straight price downgrade with the same core features?**
MeetGeek is the closest — cheaper at every published tier than Fireflies while still covering multilingual transcription and basic AI summaries, though its integration list is narrower.
